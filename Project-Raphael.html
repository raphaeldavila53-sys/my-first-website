Izuku Midoriya

About

Izuku Midoriya, also known by his hero name Deku, is a student in Class 1-A at U.A. High School. Born Quirkless, his courage and determination impressed All Might, who passed down the power of One For All to him.

Izuku Midoriya in his U.A. High School hero suit preparing for combat.

Characteristics

  1. Analytical and highly observant about Quirk abilities.
  2. Unwavering courage and selfless instinct to save others.
  3. Inheritor of the powerful One For All Quirk.
  4. Extremely hardworking and dedicated student.
