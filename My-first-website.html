About Agent Jones

Originally, Agent John Jones was an elite IO member tasked with maintaining The Loop and keeping the Zero Point under IO control . He was the face of IO’s bureaucracy, traveling between realities to recruit top hunters. His powers to traverse dimensions came with the cost of ageless immortality
